Add to Home Screen first

To register this device, add Attendance Tracker to your Home Screen, then open the app from the icon.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  3. Open Attendance Tracker from the new icon on your Home Screen.
Registering inside the app icon keeps your device registration from being lost.

Register this device

Enter the registration code provided by your administrator to connect this device to your employee account.

Your registration code is temporary and can only be used once. If you have registered before and are seeing this screen again, ask your administrator to reset your device.
